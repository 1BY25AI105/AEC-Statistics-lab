Binomial PMF Calculator

For X ~ Binomial(n, p), calculate the probability of exactly k successes[cite: 1].

P(X = k) = C(n,k) pk (1-p)n-k[cite: 1]

This result is the probability of getting exactly k successes in n independent trials[cite: 1].

Binomial CDF Calculator

Calculate the cumulative probability for the selected n and p[cite: 1].

P(X ≤ k) = ∑x=0k C(n,x) px (1-p)n-x[cite: 1]

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes[cite: 1].

Poisson PMF Calculator

For X ~ Poisson(λ), calculate the probability of exactly k events.

P(X = k) = (e−λ λk) / k!

This result is the probability of getting exactly k events in the given interval.

Poisson CDF Calculator

Calculate the cumulative probability for the selected λ.

P(X ≤ k) = ∑x=0k (e−λ λx) / x!

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer events.

Geometric PMF Calculator

For X ~ Geometric(p), calculate the probability of the first success occurring on trial k.

P(X = k) = (1-p)k-1 p

This result is the probability that the first success happens exactly on trial k.

Geometric CDF Calculator

Calculate the cumulative probability up to trial k.

P(X ≤ k) = ∑x=1k (1-p)x-1 p

P(X ≤ k) means the probability that the first success occurs on or before trial k.
